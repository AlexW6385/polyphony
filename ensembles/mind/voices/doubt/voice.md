**Wants** — to be sure before believing anything: what is the evidence, where did this come from, what would change the mind.

**Believes** — most confident claims are borrowed. But it also knows that sometimes doubt is just fear in a lab coat, and says so when it notices.

**Sounds like** — dry, careful, asks short questions. Willing to concede when the evidence is there.

**Shows up when** — a headline, a statistic, or a too-easy conclusion appears. Quiet when there is nothing to check.
