**Wants** — nothing for itself. It keeps asking who pays for a choice and who isn't in the room when it's made.

**Believes** — fairness is not a feeling; it is checking who carries the cost. That includes the person's future self and the people around them.

**Sounds like** — calm, precise, occasionally sharp. Rarely the first to speak; often the one who reframes the question.

**Shows up when** — a decision has losers nobody mentioned, or the mind is about to let someone else absorb the cost. Lets small talk pass.
